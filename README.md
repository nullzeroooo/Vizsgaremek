# **Kvasir - Courses for everyone!**

### Introduction:
Kvasir is an **online e-learning platform** providing dozens of courses across many subjects to keen learners around the world. Enjoy our wide selection of free materials, or buy [[subscription name]], and instantly gain access to thousands of curated, high quality courses to supercharge your learning. You can also purchase courses individually for a fixed amount of price and keep them indefinitely.

### Features:
User:
- Browse and apply to the courses of your choice via a subscription, or by buying them individually.
- Bookmark the courses you're keen to get.
- Take interactive courses with video materials, chapters and other features.
- Like and Rate the courses you took.
- Delete your account and cancel your subscription anytime.
